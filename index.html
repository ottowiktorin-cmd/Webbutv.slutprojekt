The Tartarus Engine

We at REMTECH would like to present our newest project: The Tartarus Engine.

What is the Tartarus Engine?

The engine is a form of penitance machine designed for max security prisons.

How it works

The Engine works by placing the inmate in a dreamlike comatose state, placing the prisoner in a simulated state of awareness. The inmate will percieve time at a slower rate compared to those outside the engine, due to the subconscous mind lacking a linear perception of time.

Installation process

The Tartarus Engine comes preinstalled with one simulation preset, however the simulation can be modified to offer any desired environments for an additional fee.

System requirements

The system requires 12x4x8 meters at minimum for the central computer, but the pods for holding inmates only require a diameter of 1.5 meters and a height of 3 meters.

Price range

We offer a variety of packages ranging from private business level and state level.
