REMTECH was founded in the early 1980's as a response to the increased demand for electric technology following the invention of the internet. REMTECH has been the leading company in the field of subconscious brain activity and related technology since the 2000s.

REMTECH's parent company, Bohrmetal, started out as a military contractor in the 1950s. Founded by Remmel Bohrmann and cofounded by Otto Skorzeny, The company signed a multitude of contracts with the US military and private contractors such as Blackwater. The unpredicted involvement of the US in global conflicts generated a fortune in company earnings.

But Bohrmann wasn't satisfied yet. While Otto retired to a quiet life in Spain, Remmel invested his own share of the profit into the continued research conducted by REMTECH.

The company's main focus was the research of artificial intelligence, but attention was quickly shifted towards researching the human mind when revolutionizing breakthroughs were made using brain scan technology.
